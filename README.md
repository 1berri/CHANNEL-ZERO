
# CHANNEL ZERO
### *Some things were never meant to be played back.*

In 2025, a temporary job digitizing archived VHS tapes leads you to the abandoned headquarters of **Morrow Media Group**, a once-prominent children's television production company that mysteriously shut down in 1997.

What begins as a routine overnight shift quickly becomes something far stranger.

Buried among decades of forgotten recordings are tapes that shouldn't exist—broadcasts dated years after Morrow's closure, footage of rooms you've never entered, and familiar characters behaving in ways they were never meant to. The deeper you dig into the station's history, the more it becomes clear that Morrow's final broadcast wasn't the end.

Explore the abandoned studios, restore damaged recordings, uncover forgotten documents, and piece together the truth behind the disappearance of Morrow Media Group.

But some recordings don't just preserve the past.

**Some of them are still watching.**

> Welcome to CHANNEL ZERO.
